Accessibility

Last updated August 22, 2026

The Jitz Guild is committed to making thejitsguild.com accessible to as many people as possible, including those who use assistive technologies.

What we're doing

  • Descriptive alternative text on meaningful images.
  • Semantic structure and keyboard-navigable menus.
  • Color contrast and legible typography we continue to test and improve.
  • Respect for reduced-motion preferences.

We're working toward conformance with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA. This is an ongoing effort as we finish building the store.

Need help or found a barrier?

If you have trouble using any part of the Site, email hello@thejitsguild.com and we'll help and prioritize a fix.
